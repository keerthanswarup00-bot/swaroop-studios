A lifetime behind the camera.

Swaroop Studios — since 1972.

"Photographs should feel like memories, not just pictures."

Photography has never simply been about taking a picture. For Swaroop, it has always been about being present when something happens — noticing the expression, the gesture, the people and the atmosphere that might otherwise disappear.

That background spans traditional studio portraiture and press assignments, and then decades of weddings, celebrations and corporate work — so every assignment is approached the same way: observant, unhurried, and documentary in spirit.

The studio is based in Bengaluru and takes on work across Bengaluru, Hoskote and the wider Karnataka region, travelling to venues as the assignment requires.

Common questions.

A few things people ask before getting in touch.

Which areas do you cover?

The studio is based in Bengaluru and takes on work across Bengaluru, Hoskote and the wider Karnataka region, travelling to venues as the assignment requires.

Do you shoot weddings and events outside the city?

Yes. Weddings, events and corporate assignments are photographed wherever they are held, so the crew travels to the venue.

Do you provide films as well as stills?

Wedding films are available alongside photography, and the archive includes studio films from past assignments.

How do we book?

Send an enquiry with your dates and what you have in mind using the form, or call, email or WhatsApp the studio directly.

Do you handle confidential or VIP assignments?

Yes. NDAs can be signed on request, images are never shared or published without written approval, and delivery can be private and password-protected.

Can portraits be done in the studio?

Studio portraiture, executive headshots and personal branding sessions are available in the Bengaluru studio.
